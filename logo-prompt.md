Design a logo for **Yuja (유자)**, a Korean-learning browser extension. Yuja is the Korean yuzu citron. The app colors words you know and don't know, and lets you click them for a dictionary card while you watch Korean videos.

## Feel
- **Refreshing:** cold citrus, sparkling water. Bright and a bit juicy.
- **Aegyo (애교):** cute and friendly, like a Korean character sticker or a Kakao/Line emoji. Soft, round, happy. Homemade doodle charm, but not generic clip art or a generic lemon.
- It sits next to a lineup of fruit themes (🍋 yuja, 🍓 strawberry, 🍇 green grape, 🍑 peach, 🫐 blueberry), so the yuja should feel like the lead character of that family.

## The mark
- One round yuja fruit with **one leaf**. Optionally give it a minimal face: two dot eyes and small blush marks, nothing more. A mouth is optional and should be at most a tiny curve.
- The silhouette is the brand. It has to read as "a cute round citrus with a leaf" even as a solid single-color blob.
- **Hand-drawn, MS Paint style:** a thick, even marker outline with a slight wobble, flat paint-bucket fills, slightly imperfect and charming, like a doodle drawn with a mouse. Not polished vector art.
- One white highlight blob for the shine. No gradients, no textures, no pore dots.
- The 16 px version will be redrawn by hand as pixel art, which fits the MS Paint look, so prioritize a bold, simple silhouette over fine detail.
- No text inside the icon mark.

## It must survive tiny sizes
This is a Chrome toolbar icon, so it has to work at **16×16, 32×32, 48×48 and 128×128 px**:
- Bold, closed shapes. The outline is thick; no thin lines or details smaller than ~1/16 of the canvas.
- At most 4 flat colors in the core mark, plus the highlight.
- Strong contrast between the fruit and the leaf, and against both light and dark browser toolbars. Use a darker rim or outline if needed.
- Faces must stay legible at 16 px. If the eyes would turn to mud, design a 16 px variant without the face or with chunkier eyes.
- Fill the square well: the fruit should take up about 80–90% of the canvas.

## Palette (from the app's design tokens)
- Yuja yellow: `#ffc21a` main, `#ffe680` highlight, `#f5a800` shade
- Leaf green: `#8cc63f`, darker `#4caf50`
- Ink (eyes, outline): `#2f4424` (a deep green-black, warmer than pure black)
- Blush: a soft peach-pink, e.g. `#ff9b8a`

## Deliverables
1. **Three distinct concepts**, each shown at 128, 48, 32 and 16 px side by side, so legibility is visible.
2. For the chosen direction:
   - A large square **PNG** (1024 px or more) on a transparent or white background.
   - A **one-color version** (single fill) to prove the silhouette.
   - A **wordmark lockup**: icon + "유자 Yuja" in a rounded, friendly typeface (think Nunito / BM HANNA style). The Hangul and Latin should feel balanced.
3. A sentence per concept explaining how it stays recognizable at 16 px.

## Avoid
- Generic lemon or orange clip art, photorealism, 3D renders, slick corporate vector polish.
- Drop shadows, glows or gradients that blur at small sizes.
- Busy details: seeds, segments, stems plus leaves plus flowers, sparkle clusters.
- Anything that reads as a smiley-face emoji first and fruit second.
